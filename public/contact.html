FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you find candidates?
For temporary roles, we can provide candidates within 24-48 hours. For permanent positions, typically 7-14 days depending on the role and sector.
What areas do you cover?
We cover the entire United Kingdom, from Scotland to the South Coast. Our consultants have local knowledge in all major regions.
How much does your service cost?
Our fees are competitive and transparent. We offer different pricing models including contingency, retained, and volume discounts. Contact us for a tailored quote.
Do you offer guarantees?
Yes, all permanent placements come with a 12-month guarantee. If the candidate leaves within the first year, we'll find a replacement at no additional cost.
